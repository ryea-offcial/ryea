WELCOME TO RYEA

We, the Rohingya youths, born into displacement yet driven by hope, unite with a shared determination to reclaim our dignity, strengthen our communities, and build a future rooted in justice, knowledge, and self-reliance. Despite statelessness, persecution, and prolonged displacement, we refuse to be defined by victimhood. This manifesto sets forth our collective vision, mission, principles and commitments for empowering Rohingya youths as agents of positive change.

Our Vision:

"A resilient, educated, and self-empowered Rohingya youth community that upholds human dignity, contributes constructively to society, and plays a leading role in shaping a peaceful and sustainable future for the Rohingya people."

Our Mission:

"To empower Rohingya youths through education, leadership development, skills training, advocacy, and community service, enabling them to become confident leaders, responsible citizens, and advocates for justice and human rights."
