WELCOME TO RHI

RHI is dedicated to providing humanitarian aid, education, and healthcare for the Rohingya community, who have faced unprecedented challenges and displacement. Join us in our journey to uplift and empower one of the most vulnerable communities in the world.

Mission:

"To empower and support the Rohingya community through sustainable humanitarian aid, educational programs, and healthcare for their rights and dignity."

Vision:

"A world where the Rohingya people thrive with dignity, equality, and access to all opportunities for a fulfilling life."

Educational Initiatives

RHI unites hearts and minds, fostering hope and empowerment through impactful education. Our mission is to uplift and empower, bringing about a transformative change in the lives of the Rohingya.
